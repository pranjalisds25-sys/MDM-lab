Frequently Asked Questions

Click on a question to view or hide its answer.

Students can apply for admission through the official college admission portal. Fill in the application form, upload the required documents and submit the application before the specified deadline.
The examination timetable is available on the college website and student portal. Students should regularly check the notice section for updates.
College fees can be paid through the online student portal using the available payment options. Students may also contact the accounts department for assistance.
Students can collect their ID cards from the college administration office after completing the required registration process.
Click the "Forgot Password" option on the student portal login page and follow the instructions to reset your password. You can contact the IT department if you need additional help.
You can contact the college help desk during working hours through the official phone number or email address. You can also visit the help desk in the administration building.
